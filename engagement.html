Public Engagement

I am actively involved in public engagement, professional training, and institutional work on AI — contributing to discussions in schools, professional associations, companies, trade unions, cultural events, and public institutions. At the University of Bergamo, this includes a university-wide training cycle on the use of AI for faculty and technical-administrative staff.

Research seminars and conference presentations are under Research Talks.
